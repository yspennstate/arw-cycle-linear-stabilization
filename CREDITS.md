# Credits

The question is posed in L. T. Rolla, *Activated random walks on Z^d*, Probab. Surv. 17 (2020), 478-544.

The paper rests on the following work.

- R. Basu, S. Ganguly, C. Hoffman and J. Richey, *Activated random walk on a cycle*, Ann. Inst. Henri Poincare
  Probab. Stat. 55 (2019), 1258-1277: the two phases of activated random walk on the cycle.
- T. Johnson and J. Richey, *The odometer in subcritical activated random walk*, Electron. Commun. Probab. 31 (2026),
  paper no. 74: the stable odometers on Z that the proof pastes onto the cycle.
- C. Hoffman, T. Johnson and M. Junge, *The density conjecture for activated random walk*, arXiv:2406.01731 (2024):
  the identification of the critical density, and the earlier n log^2 n bound for a fixed number of particles.
- B. N. B. de Lima, L. T. Rolla and C. Terra, *On the slow phase for fixed-energy activated random walks*, J. Appl.
  Probab. 63 (2026), 1330-1345.
- L. Levine and F. Liang, *Exact sampling and fast mixing of activated random walk*, Electron. J. Probab. 29 (2024);
  A. Bristiel and J. Salez, *Separation cutoff for activated random walks*, Ann. Appl. Probab. 34 (2024): mixing of
  the driven-dissipative chain.

The full list of references is in the paper.
